Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly; at the assessment we decide whether any tests or a referral are needed.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your case. After the assessment, we suggest a plan with an estimated number of sessions.

Do you treat patients at home?

Yes, in the clinic's area, depending on availability.

Can I reschedule my session?

Yes, from your Google Calendar, on a real website.

What should I bring to my first session?

Comfortable clothes and, if you have them, any reports or scans related to your pain.
