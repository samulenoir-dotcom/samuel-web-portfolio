About me

I am Valeria Torres, a life and business coach. I work with people who already know what they want and need structure, consistency, and someone to help them stay the course.

My job is not to hand you ready-made answers. It is to help you think more clearly, choose what to do first, and turn that decision into a weekly habit.

Listen

Judgment-free sessions, with questions that bring order.

Plan

Clear goals, small steps, and real dates.

Follow-up

Weekly review of progress and obstacles.

How I work, in 3 steps

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A free session to meet, understand your situation, and decide if the 1:1 program is right for you.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We build your plan together: goal, weekly steps, and how we will measure progress. No magic promises, just concrete work.

  3. 03

    Support

    Weekly sessions and follow-up between sessions. We review what worked, what got stuck, and adjust the plan.

Who is it for?

For you if you have had a goal on your mind for months and want to get it organized with someone by your side. It is not for you if you are looking for a quick fix or for someone to decide for you: here, you do the work, I walk with you.

Frequently asked questions

Does the discovery session cost anything?

No. It is 30 minutes online, free and with no commitment, so we can meet and see if we work well together.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online, by video call, in your time zone. Group workshops are announced with a date and location.

What if I cannot make my booked time?

You can reschedule from your Google Calendar, on a real website.

Does coaching replace therapy?

No. Coaching works on goals and habits. If your topic needs therapeutic support, I will tell you in the first session.

How do I pay for the 1:1 program?

We agree on it after the discovery session, based on the length of the program.

Shall we start with a discovery session?

30 minutes, online, free. No commitment.
