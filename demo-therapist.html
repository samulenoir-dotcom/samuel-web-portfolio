What a session looks like

You talk, the therapist listens, and together you make sense of what you bring. No homework you didn't agree to, no judgment — the space is yours.

  • 50 min

    Duration. 50-minute sessions, once a week or every two weeks, as you agree together.

  • Online

    In person or online. At the office or over a private video call, with the same care and confidentiality.

  • Private

    Confidentiality. Everything said in session stays in session.

  • Clear

    No surprises. Fees and frequency are agreed at your first session, before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I share in session confidential?

Yes. Everything said in session stays in session, in person or online.

Can I have sessions from another country?

Yes, online, in your time zone.

Does online therapy work the same?

Yes. It's the same 50-minute session, over a private video call, from your home.

How often are sessions?

Usually once a week or every two weeks. Frequency is agreed with you.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, with advance notice, from your Google Calendar, on a real website.

Is there any commitment in the first session?

No. It's a first conversation to get to know each other and see if we work well together.
